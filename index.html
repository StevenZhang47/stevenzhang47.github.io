Splash at Yale · E5492 · 2026

Can a Computer Taste?

Machine Learning Through Food

A hands-on introduction to machine learning for students in grades 6–12. Working in pairs, students taste foods, rate sweetness, saltiness, sourness, and enjoyment, and turn their own responses into a dataset.

The lesson moves from guessing model weights to fitting linear regression, testing on wasabi peas, and building a small neural network in Google Colab. Students ask whether seeing some wasabi examples helps a model predict the responses kept aside for testing.
